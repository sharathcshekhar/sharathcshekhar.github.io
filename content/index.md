I am a **Software Architect and Systems Researcher** with over 10 years of industry experience and a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [University at Buffalo](http://buffalo.edu/). I currently work as a **Staff Research Engineer II** in the [Digital Health Lab](https://sra.samsung.com/research-area/digital-health/) at **Samsung Research America** in Silicon Valley, CA.

My work spans **distributed cloud systems, digital health platforms, and GenAI for personalized wellness**. Most recently, I lead systems and infrastructure strategy, architecting modular, event-driven frameworks for LLM-based applications, prompt management, and high-availability distributed AI components.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at UB, advised by [Dr. Steve Ko](https://steveyko.github.io/teaching/) and [Dr. Karthik Dantu](https://dkkarthik.github.io/), where I led the [BlueMountain](research.html#bluemountain) and [Cider](research.html#cider) (collaborating with [Dr. Vipin Chaudhary](https://case.edu/engineering/about/faculty-and-staff-directory/vipin-chaudhary)) systems research projects. Before that, I worked on the database engines team of **Amazon Web Services (Redshift)**, and developed enterprise storage controller software at **NetApp**.
