When I am not doing research and run out of beer, I am found brewing my own!

I love craft beer and turned that passion into a rewarding creative hobby through homebrewing. I am a **nanoscale brewer**, crafting experimental beers in small batches of 1–2 gallons. I primarily work with high-quality malt extracts and incorporate partial mashing to dial in complex grain bills.

I use reliable dry yeasts, explore diverse hop schedules, and enjoy pushing the boundaries beyond rigid style guidelines (which explains experimental batches like my sweet Orange Hefeweizen!).

---

### Competition & Recognition

In 2017, I entered my first homebrew competition, **[Amber Waves of Grains](http://awog.niagarabrewers.org)** with my **[Dubbel Trouble](recipes.html#dubbel)** (BJCP Category 26B).

Competing against experienced Western New York brewers, the Belgian Dubbel scored **33.5 / 50 ("Very Good")**, just shy of the "Excellent" (38+) threshold! You can view the competition score sheet [here (PDF)](assets/awog-2017-results.pdf).
