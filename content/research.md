My research focuses on the design, implementation, and evaluation of computer systems. My core strength lies in bringing architectural structure to nascent research challenges—transforming complex ideas into stable, scalable, and production-ready systems.

My work spans **GenAI systems and agentic frameworks for personalized wellness**, **HIPAA-compliant digital health platforms**, and **flexible mobile storage architectures**.

## Areas & Projects

### GenAI for Wellness & Agentic Frameworks

At Samsung Research America, I lead systems and infrastructure strategy evaluating foundational models, prompt management systems, RAG pipelines, LLM guardrails, and agentic workflows for health and wellness applications across mobile and wearable platforms.

### Digital Health & Wearable Biomarkers

In the [Digital Health Lab](https://sra.samsung.com/research-area/digital-health/) at Samsung Research America, architecting end-to-end data collection and processing platforms for healthcare research and clinical trials, including ML-driven biomarker detection and dynamic multi-sensor fusion from wearable and earbud sensors (e.g., *MAD-Fusion* modality-aware dynamic fusion in IEEE IoTJ '26, *MindfulBuddy* breathing biofeedback in IEEE IoTJ '25, *Cough-DL* ear-worn detection in IEEE EMBC '24).

### BlueMountain: Flexible Data Management on Mobile Systems

BlueMountain introduces unprecedented flexibility for mobile data management by decoupling application logic from storage and sync policies, advised by [Dr. Steven Y. Ko](https://steveyko.github.io/teaching/) and [Dr. Karthik Dantu](https://dkkarthik.github.io/). It enables users to download unmodified apps and customize data storage mechanisms (local encryption, cloud sync providers, retention policies) seamlessly.

- **Status**: Published in [ACM MobiCom '17](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3086467.3086474), ACM MobiSys '17 Demo, and USENIX HotStorage '15.
- **Videos**: [Presentation Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GzO6vzpuhpE) · [Demo & Teaser Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZKiU0_cSOts)

### Cider: Block-Level Variable Redundancy on Distributed Flash Arrays

Cider is a high-performance, scalable virtual block device supporting temporally variable data redundancy, developed in collaboration with [Dr. Vipin Chaudhary](https://case.edu/engineering/about/faculty-and-staff-directory/vipin-chaudhary). Operating as a distributed block store with erasure coding, Cider controls data redundancy at single-block granularity across flash arrays.

- **Status**: Published in IEEE ICCCN '17; Patent Application WO2015161140A1.

### Pixelsior: Photo Management as a Platform Service

A mobile photo management framework that transparently handles content adaptation, caching policies, and image manipulation across apps.

- **Status**: Published in USENIX HotStorage '16 (collaborative work led by Kyungho Jeon).

### PigOut: Federated Data Processing Across Hadoop Clusters

Coordinates federated big-data analytics and query execution across multiple geographically distributed Hadoop clusters.

- **Status**: Published in IEEE BigData '14.
